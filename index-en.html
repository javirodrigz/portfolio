Organizational change · e-Learning

Remote Work

I designed an experience to support employees who transitioned to remote work and translate Mexican workplace guidance into everyday decisions after the pandemic.

A sample of the course and its audiovisual treatment.

Challenge
Turn a broad regulatory requirement into relevant guidance for people working from home.
Audience
Employees in Mexico working remotely.
My contribution
As Senior Learning Designer, I contributed to project coordination and developed the experience design, content structure, and visual production for the e-learning course.
Design decision
I organized the content around the realities of remote work so the regulation would feel recognizable and actionable.
View process and evidence +

Process
I used SAM to iterate on the solution and developed it in Captivate with assets produced in Adobe Creative Suite.

What I would measure
Understanding of self-care practices, recognition of risks, and use of the available support resources.

Conversation skills · Multilingual e-Learning

Feedback

I designed a multilingual experience to help leaders and employees understand feedback as a practical, structured, and useful skill.

A sample of the course and its interactions. The published samples do not include every language version developed.

Challenge
Turn a broad interpersonal concept into guidance that could be used during real conversations.
Audience
Employees and leaders who give and receive feedback.
My contribution
As Senior Learning Designer, I contributed to project coordination and developed the instructional design, multilingual adaptation, animation, and interactions.
Design decision
I combined the STAR method with discovery and reflection activities so learners could practice before a real conversation.
View process and evidence +

Process
I applied ADDIE and developed the experience with Captivate and Adobe Creative Suite.

What I would measure
Recognition of the STAR method, confidence to start the conversation, and application of a clear structure.

How do I work?

From a business challenge
to a solution that can be
implemented and improved.

  1. Diagnose

    I identify the audience, work context, performance gap, and expected behavior before recommending a response.

  2. Align and plan

    I define objectives, scope, timelines, deliverables, and responsibilities with managers, SMEs, and the teams involved.

  3. Design

    I connect practice, content, and evaluation, and make the solution visible early to validate the journey.

  4. Coordinate and deliver

    I track tasks and progress, present project status, and manage changes alongside the manager or LBP.

  5. Evaluate and learn

    I define signals of understanding and application, test the solution, and document lessons for the next iteration.
